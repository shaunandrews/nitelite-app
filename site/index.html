Pick how the screen cuts to black

Five transitions ship with the app. In the room above, each one is a thing that makes the dark. In NiteLite, right-click the menu bar icon to choose.

  • Classic film iris

    The silent-film ending. A circle closes on the middle of the screen, then opens on the other mode. In the room: the projector.

  • Left-right wipe

    A soft-edged band of black sweeps across the screen and carries on out. In the room: the door.

  • Old TV turn-off

    The picture squeezes to a line, shrinks to a glowing dot, and blinks out. In the room: the TV.

  • Venetian blinds

    Fourteen slats slide shut from alternating sides. In the room: the blinds.

  • Curtain call

    Two heavy curtains swing shut, then part on the other mode. In the room: the curtains.

Install it in a minute

  1. Download NiteLite and open the zip.
  2. Drag NiteLite into your Applications folder, then open it.
  3. NiteLite isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
  4. When asked, let NiteLite control System Events. That's the permission it uses to flip the Dark Mode setting.

Press ⌘ ⇧ D anywhere to switch. To have it start with your Mac, add it under System Settings, General, Login Items.
