NiteLite Download for Mac

Let there be night.

NiteLite is a free menu bar app that switches your Mac between Light and Dark Mode, with a film cut in between.

  • Click it in your menu bar
  • Or press ⌘⇧D from any app
  • Made for testing sites and apps in both modes
Pick a cut, then try it
Free and open source · macOS 13 or later · Source on GitHub · Made by Shaun Andrews

Install it in a minute

  1. Download NiteLite (1.8 MB) and open the zip.
  2. Drag NiteLite into your Applications folder, then open it.
  3. NiteLite isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
  4. When asked, let NiteLite control System Events. That's how it flips the Dark Mode setting.

Then press ⌘ ⇧ D anywhere, or click the menu bar icon. Right-click it to pick a cut.
